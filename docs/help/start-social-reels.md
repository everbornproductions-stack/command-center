# Start social reels

Open **Start a project** on the Command home screen, or from the Work menu. Pick **Social media reels** and click **Start**. The same steps run in Simple and in Expert. Simple keeps the route in plain words. Expert shows which AI was chosen and lets you edit the plan before it runs.

1. Describe the idea. A sentence or two is enough. Click **Continue**.
2. Answer the questions. Required: **Brand and topic** and **How many reels?** Optional: **Where will they run?** and **What should the viewer do next?** Optional questions can stay blank. Click **Continue**.
3. Click **Ask the council for a plan**. The council builds a short set of reels: a hook, a caption, and what to film. Nothing runs until you click **Build it**.
4. Read the plan. In Expert you can edit a step title or its instructions. Click **Build it**.
5. Each step goes to the best AI for that work. Progress shows on the live view and the flow map. **Open live view** is on the page.
6. On **Review**, read the result. Say what should change and click **Revise**, or click **Export**, or click **Finish**.

Export downloads a Markdown file on this computer. Nothing is posted or emailed. Film only what you are willing to post, and read each caption aloud before you use it. A paid clip waits until you click **Approve** in Media. Nothing is generated on its own.

To see a labeled sample first, click **Watch an example** on the Social media reels card. That sample is Milo's Guitar. See Watch example projects. To walk the rest of the console again, see Take the tour.
