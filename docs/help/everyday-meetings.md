# How to prep a meeting and summarize a transcript

Open **Docs and follow-up** in the Work menu.

Try saying: Prep me for my 2pm

Try saying: Summarize yesterday's call and list to-dos

1. Save the meeting title, the time, and your notes. Prep me reads related docs on this computer.
2. Paste a transcript. The summary keeps the first three sentences and lists lines that look like action items.
3. **Add the to-dos** is a separate click. Until then nothing is added.
4. This build does not record calls.

Prep me for my 2pm. A transcript summary lists action items. Adding those to-dos is a separate click. This build does not record calls.
