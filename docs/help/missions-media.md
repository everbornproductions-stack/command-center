# Missions, media, costs, and approvals

## What a mission is

Mission: a planner splits the goal, specialists work in parallel, a different AI checks each piece, and an assembler compiles the result.

A mission can include a video, an image, or a voice clip. Meldmind shows the estimate first. A paid step waits until you click Approve. Nothing is spent on that step before Approve.

The same rule covers files you drop into a project. You see what will be read and the cost. Nothing is added until you confirm.

In Expert, the cap on the order box stops a mission from spending more than you allow. Spend caps in Settings do the same for a provider across the month. Simple still uses your saved mission cap. It just does not draw the cap controls on the chat box.

If you decline, the paid step does not run.

## Pause, edit, and resume

Pro can pause a mission, edit the steps that have not finished, and resume. Done steps stay as they are. Run in background leaves this screen. You get an alert when it is done. Nothing is emailed.
