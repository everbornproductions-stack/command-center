# Plain requests

Try saying: email this client and put it on my calendar.

Type that in the plain request box on Chat. A cheap fast AI drafts the email and the event. You see the cost, usually a fraction of a cent.

Meldmind sends the email through the Gmail account you connected and creates the event on Google Calendar. That happens only after you click Approve once. The AI that wrote the draft does not touch your accounts. Grok Bot is not used for this.

## Before you approve

Connect Gmail and tick send email. Connect Google Calendar. If either one is missing, Approve does not send.

You can edit the To address, the subject, the body, and the event before you approve. If the request did not include an email address, type one. Nothing is invented for you.

## What the click does

Approve sends the email and creates the event together. Draft only writes the preview and records the small model cost. It does not email anyone and it does not change your calendar.

A second click on a finished request does not send the email again.
