# How to turn an email into a task or a calendar hold

Open **Day plan**.

Try saying: Turn this email into a task and a calendar hold

1. Paste the from line, the subject, and the body.
2. Click **Preview**. You see the task, and a hold when the email names a day or a time.
3. Click **Save task and hold**. That click writes both on this computer.
4. Google Calendar is not changed. Remove hold undoes the local hold.

A password or a Social Security number in the paste is refused and nothing is saved.

More than 10 emails at once shows the count and a short sample of subjects before any are saved.
