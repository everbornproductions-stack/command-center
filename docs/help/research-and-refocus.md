# Research and Refocus

Research looks things up on the web and shows the pages it used. You can open a source and read it yourself. Search stays off until you turn it on. The switch is in the chat, and the settings live under Web search.

Refocus is for work you already started. When that page is in your app, you type a topic, such as half-done reels, and it lists the related chats and files from oldest to newest. Finding that list is Pro at $18.99 a month. Rebuilding it into a finished piece is Extreme at $29.99 a month when that choice is on the page. A rebuild starts only after you click.

The Help list shows Research when search is in the app, and Refocus when that page is in the app. Open uses the page that is actually here.
