# Start a business plan

Open **Start a project** on the Command home screen, or from the Work menu. Pick **Business plan** and click **Start**. The same steps run in Simple and in Expert. Simple keeps the route in plain words. Expert shows which AI was chosen and lets you edit the plan before it runs.

1. Describe the idea. A sentence or two is enough. Click **Continue**.
2. Answer the questions. Required: **The business in one sentence** and **Who pays, and for what?** Optional: **Facts you already know** and **What decision should this plan support?** Optional questions can stay blank. Empty facts stay empty. Nothing is invented to fill them. Click **Continue**.
3. Click **Ask the council for a plan**. The council builds a short plan from the facts you supplied. Missing numbers stay marked [ASK]. Nothing runs until you click **Build it**.
4. Read the plan. In Expert you can edit a step title or its instructions. Click **Build it**.
5. Each step goes to the best AI for that work. Progress shows on the live view and the flow map. **Open live view** is on the page.
6. On **Review**, read the result. Say what should change and click **Revise**, or click **Export**, or click **Finish**.

Export downloads a Markdown file on this computer. Nothing is posted or emailed. Do not treat [ASK] as a real number. The draft is not sent to a bank or a partner. Figures in an example project are not yours.

To see a labeled sample first, click **Watch an example** on the Business plan card. That sample is Bright Kettle. See Watch example projects. To walk the rest of the console again, see Take the tour.
