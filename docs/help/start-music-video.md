# Start a music video

Open **Start a project** on the Command home screen, or from the Settings menu. Pick **Music video** and click **Start**. The same steps run in Simple and in Expert. Simple keeps the route in plain words. Expert shows which AI was chosen and lets you edit the plan before it runs.

1. Describe the idea. A sentence or two is enough. Click **Continue**.
2. Answer the questions. Required: **Song title, and what it is about** and **Look and mood**. Optional: **Who is on camera?** and **Length and where it will be posted**. Optional questions can stay blank. Click **Continue**.
3. Click **Ask the council for a plan**. The council builds a concept, a shot list, and prompts you can take to a video tool. Nothing runs until you click **Build it**.
4. Read the plan. In Expert you can edit a step title or its instructions. Click **Build it**.
5. Each step goes to the best AI for that work. Progress shows on the live view and the flow map. **Open live view** is on the page.
6. On **Review**, read the result. Say what should change and click **Revise**, or click **Export**, or click **Finish**.

Export downloads a Markdown file on this computer. Nothing is posted or emailed. Approve the shot list before you film. A paid video step waits until you click **Approve**. Nothing is generated on its own. Keep wardrobe and light the same from shot to shot.

To see a labeled sample first, click **Watch an example** on the Music video card. That sample is Lumen Park and the song Glass Orchard. See Watch example projects. To walk the rest of the console again, see Replay the tour.
