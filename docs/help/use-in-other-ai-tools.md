# Use Meldmind inside your other AI tools

Open Settings and find Use Meldmind inside your other AI tools. Pick the tool, press Copy, and paste the lines where the box says. Meldmind does not change anything on your computer. Your other tool can then ask Meldmind to run a team of AIs, and Meldmind keeps your spending limit and saves the results to MindMedia.

The lines name a token file on this computer. They never contain the token itself.
