# How to draft a new lead follow-up

Open **Docs and follow-up** in the Work menu. This action is Pro.

Try saying: When a new HighLevel lead comes in, draft a text and email

1. Enter the name, an email, a phone number, and a short note. Click **Draft the follow-up**.
2. HighLevel is not called. The drafts stay on this computer.
3. Read the email and the text. They show the exact words.
4. **Save email draft** and **Save text draft** are separate clicks. One click saves one channel into the outbox on this computer. Nothing is sent: copy the words into your own email or text app, or use HighLevel with its preview and Send anyway steps.
5. Twilio charges the user's account, but this build does not send a text or look up a price.

When a new HighLevel lead comes in, draft a text and email. Send is one click for one channel. Twilio was not contacted and no price was looked up.
