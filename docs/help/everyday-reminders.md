# How to add a reminder or a to-do in plain text

Open **Inbox and tasks**. The Add in plain text box is under the summary.

## Reminder

Try saying: Remind me at 5 to call Mom

1. Type that line, or your own.
2. Click **Add**.
3. A clock in the words (at 5, tomorrow, a weekday, or next month) saves a reminder on this computer.
4. At 5 with no am or pm means 5 in the evening. If that time today has passed, it is tomorrow.

## To-do

Try saying: Remind me to renew my license next month

1. Type the line and click **Add**.
2. Next month gives it a date. A line with no time is a to-do.
3. Click **Done** when it is finished, or **Remove** to undo it.

Nothing is emailed from this box. More than 10 lines at once asks you to confirm, and shows how many there are plus a short sample, before any of them are saved.

Do not put a password or a Social Security number in a reminder. The page refuses that line.
