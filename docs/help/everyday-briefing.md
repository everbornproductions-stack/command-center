# How to run a morning briefing

Open **Day plan** in the Work menu.

Try saying: Every weekday at 8: agenda, inbox, tasks, weather, and news

1. Click **Save weekday 8:00 routine**. Free includes one routine. A second one asks for Pro.
2. Add a city if you want the weather line to name a place. Weather is not fetched.
3. Click **Prepare now**, or leave the app open so the routine can prepare after 8:00 on a weekday.
4. Read the agenda, inbox note, tasks, weather line, and news line. Sample headlines are labeled sample.

The briefing does not send email, book anything, or post. Outgoing mail from Find a time still waits for Send.
