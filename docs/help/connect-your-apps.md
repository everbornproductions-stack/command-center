# Connect your apps

You can connect the apps you already use so Meldmind can work with them.

## Google and Microsoft: one click

- **Sign in with Google** connects Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive.
- **Sign in with Microsoft** connects Outlook.

Both buttons are on the welcome screen, in Get everything connected, and in Settings. The sign-in page opens in your normal browser. You pick your account there. Your password never reaches Meldmind. The sign-in token stays in the vault on this computer.

The first sign-in asks for as little as it can:

- Google: calendar events, Drive files that Meldmind makes or that you pick, and sending email.
- Microsoft: sending Outlook email and Outlook calendar events.
- Both also ask for your name and email address, so Meldmind can show which account is signed in. That gives no access to your mail, files, or calendar.

Every email still waits for your OK before it goes.

Reading your mail is a separate sign-in. Press **Read my inbox** (Google) or **Read my Outlook inbox** (Microsoft) only if you want Meldmind to read mail, for example to find receipts or sum up your inbox. Meldmind only reads. It cannot delete or change mail.

If a button says one-click sign-in is coming soon, Everborn's sign-in app is not in this copy yet. Developers can use their own sign-in app under **Advanced** (Settings, Browser helper for Google; Subscriptions for Outlook).

## Other apps

- Zapier and Make, when that page is in your app
- MCP, under Settings, then Tools (MCP). In Simple, switch to Expert to see that panel.
- Webhooks, on the Schedules page, and on the Zapier page when that page is here

Free includes one connected service. Pro is $18.99 a month and includes more. Extreme is $29.99 a month.

A webhook or a connected app can start work. A result that would be sent somewhere waits until you approve it. Nothing goes out until you say so. Keys and tokens stay on this computer.
