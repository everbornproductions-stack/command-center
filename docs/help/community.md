# Community and AI News

Community is in the Business menu. In Simple it is also under Help.

Community chat has channels for General, Prompts, Builders, Help, and Show and tell. Sign in with your licence email and a display name. Click Send to post. Keys and private files are refused.

AI News sits next to those channels. It updates weekly from Supabase, so a new issue does not need an app update. It lists model releases, price changes, and new features from major AI providers, each with a source link, plus Meldmind features, top voted prompts, and community highlights. An unread badge shows until you open it. You can react and share an item into chat. A draft waits for the owner before it is published.

Email me the weekly note is off until you tick it in Settings or during setup. The app writes the note and does not send email. The note has an unsubscribe link and an Everborn Productions address placeholder.

## How to join

Use the email on your licence. Pick a display name of 2 to 24 characters. Nothing is posted until you click Send.

Share a prompt, a builder, or a mission with Share in chat. That click is the post. If you have not joined, the app opens Community and does not post.

Shared prompts can get a thumbs up or thumbs down. Those votes feed the prompt library ranking.

## Keeping it safe

Be kind. Stay on the channel topic. No keys, private files, or spam. Use Report if a post breaks this. The owner can remove a post or mute someone. You can delete your own post.

A message that looks like a key, a private file path, or spam is refused.

## AI News

Open AI News next to the chat channels. A New badge means you have not opened the latest issue. React with Up or Down, or click Share in chat to drop an item into General.

The owner can paste source JSON under More options, build a draft, and publish it. People do not see the draft. Publishing does not send email.

A generator script can also build that draft from local snapshots of provider changelogs and blogs. It writes JSON and, if you ask, an HTML and text copy. It does not publish and it does not send email.

## The email copy

The checkbox says Email me the weekly AI News note. It starts off. Tick it in Settings or on the first setup page if you want the copy. Leaving it off does not add you.

Write the email copy shows the note on screen and says it was not sent. The footer has the unsubscribe link and the Everborn Productions address placeholder.
