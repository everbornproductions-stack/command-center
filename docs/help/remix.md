# Remix

Remix rebuilds from scratch. It does not copy the app or game you show. It does not decompile, extract, or reuse that app's code, assets, names, or brand.

The page is one path.

1. What do you want to make? Use your own name.
2. Choose a starting point: a screenshot, a recording, your design file, a game type, or a description.
3. Preview who receives this.
4. Build. This rebuilds from scratch.
5. Test. This checks that the page opens and that people can use it.
6. Publish. Nothing is posted until you press Publish.

More options holds the extra choices: a browser game starter, a desktop game starter, new levels, a voice, colors and type only, a second idea, an installer, and play together.

A screenshot becomes a new layout. A recording becomes a clickable preview, and that choice is on Extreme. Your design file can be an SVG you exported. Penpot is not included. A game type names Godot and Kenney. Those starter kits are MIT and are not copied.

The browser game starter runs through its own setup tool and is not included. Its license is not confirmed, so its files are not copied. A desktop game starter (Bevy) is on Extreme.

Free art is CC0 only, from Kenney or OpenGameArt. Anything else is rejected. Every kept file is listed in CREDITS. Downloads wait until you approve them. The license tool runs as its own program and is not part of Meldmind.

A voice uses Piper or Coqui, and only after you approve it. Piper runs as its own program. It is not part of Meldmind.

Test writes a page check and a short report. The extra checkers are not included and are not started from here.

Publish can stay on this computer, go to a website, go to itch.io, or make a phone build. The phone build is on Extreme. Your token is stored with your keys. Nothing is posted until you press Publish.

Remix is a Pro mission. Pro includes 8 Remix missions a month. That number is a placeholder, not a price. Extreme has no monthly cap. A rating teaches the next remix on this computer. Nothing from that record is sent.

Your own art can become sprites, models, or voices when you ask. Media still shows a price first and waits for Approve.

## Build it all for me

Turn on **Build it all for me** to run every step in one go: what to make, the starting point, the preview, the build, and the test. Publish stays a click. Meldmind answers the questions from your description. When a question has nothing in the description, it picks a plain default and lists it under **Assumed**, so you can change it later.

It still stops for three things, the same rules as Get it done: spending money, deleting something for good, and posting or sending in public. A second switch, **Also allow posting**, lets public posting through. It is off unless you turn it on. Boxes that only you can tick, such as a compliance check, still wait for you.

You see each step as it runs, and **Stop** ends the run before the next step. The switch is off at first. Meldmind remembers your choice for next time.
