# Remix any app

Remix rebuilds from scratch. It does not copy the app or game you show. It does not decompile, extract, or reuse that app's code, assets, names, or brand.

Show what you saw: a web address, screenshots, a short screen recording, or an app on this computer. An app on this computer is observed only through what is visible on screen. Remix does not open the address, and it does not open installed files. Describe how it looks, plays, and works in your own words.

Then give it your own name, characters, art, and ideas. The AIs plan a new project from that. Free engines (Godot, Phaser), templates, and free art packs can be named from the catalog on this computer. Nothing is downloaded. Each item has a license check. If the license is missing or is not free to use, it is not used. Required credits are written to a CREDITS file in the new project.

Official mod tools for Minecraft, Roblox, Godot, and Unity are listed. Use that product's own tools. Leave the game's files alone.

Preview who receives this before you run it. The preview names the AIs and services. Nothing is sent until you click Rebuild from scratch. If the new name, look, or characters are too close to the original, Remix warns you. Saving still needs that click.

Remix is a Pro mission. Pro includes 8 Remix missions a month. That number is a placeholder, not a price. Extreme has no monthly cap. A rating teaches the next remix on this computer. Nothing from that record is sent.

Your own art can become sprites, models, or voices when you ask. Media still shows a price first and waits for Approve.
