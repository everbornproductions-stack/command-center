# Start an app

Open **Start a project** on the Command home screen, or from the Work menu. Pick **App/software** and click **Start**. The same steps run in Simple and in Expert. Simple keeps the route in plain words. Expert shows which AI was chosen and lets you edit the plan before it runs.

1. Describe the idea. A sentence or two is enough. Click **Continue**.
2. Answer the questions. Required: **Who uses it, and what job does it do?** and **What must the first version do?** Optional: **Phone, web, or desktop?** and **What does done look like for this pass?** Optional questions can stay blank. Click **Continue**.
3. Click **Ask the council for a plan**. The council builds who it is for, the must-have screens, and a build order. Nothing runs until you click **Build it**.
4. Read the plan. In Expert you can edit a step title or its instructions. Click **Build it**.
5. Each step goes to the best AI for that work. Progress shows on the live view and the flow map. **Open live view** is on the page.
6. On **Review**, read the result. Say what should change and click **Revise**, or click **Export**, or click **Finish**.

Export downloads a Markdown file on this computer. Nothing is posted or emailed. The result is an outline, not shipped software. Cut anything that is not a must-have for this pass. You start the build yourself. This walk does not deploy or ship code.

To see a labeled sample first, click **Watch an example** on the App/software card. That sample is Fieldnote. See Watch example projects. To walk the rest of the console again, see Take the tour.
