# How to summarize your inboxes and draft a reply

Open **Inbox and tasks** in the Work menu. The page shows a How to section and the phrase to try.

## Inbox summary

Try saying: What needs me today in all my inboxes?

1. Click **Try this example** to see two sample mailboxes, or click **Summarize connected inboxes** after Google is connected with read Gmail.
2. Each connected Google account is read on its own. Home and Work stay separate.
3. Newsletters are marked and left out of Needs a reply.
4. This step only reads. Nothing is sent, archived, or deleted.

An Outlook account connected for receipts is listed, and its open messages are not copied into the summary. A Gmail account used only for receipt scanning is listed the same way. The summary reads the Google sign-in that includes read Gmail.

## Reply in my tone

Try saying: Reply to Dana, say yes to Thursday

1. On a message that needs a reply, click **Draft a reply**.
2. The draft uses How to talk to me and My voice from Settings when those fields are filled in.
3. Read the To, subject, and body. Edit them if you want.
4. Click **Send**. That click sends the To, subject, and body on screen, and nothing else.
5. Send stays off until Gmail is connected with send email ticked. The draft remains until then.

Show the examples walks these phrases on the page. Take the tour again also stops on them.

Passwords and Social Security numbers are not kept in a draft.
