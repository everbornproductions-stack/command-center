# Builders

Builders are in Create. A builder shows up only when it fits what you are doing.

Describe what you need. Preview which AIs would see your words. Then Build, Test, and Publish. Nothing is sent, posted, or charged until you click Publish.

HighLevel builders show up when HighLevel is connected, or when you mention HighLevel. They cover a lead form, a text or email you approve, workflow tags, a daily pipeline summary, and page copy. A text or email asks you to confirm permission, STOP, quiet hours, A2P 10DLC, and CAN-SPAM. Credit repair copy adds a CROA note and does not promise a result.

Accounting builders show up when QuickBooks or Xero is connected, or when a receipt or an invoice comes up. Meldmind only organizes them. For a tax question it says: Ask your accountant. It does not give tax advice. Sending to QuickBooks or Xero waits for your approval and does not pay the bill. Invoice Ninja stays off unless you turn it on. It is a separate service.

A website chat, a booking page, meeting notes, a contract, or a review reply shows up from that topic. A contract uses your own DocuSeal template. DocuSeal is a separate service. This is not legal advice. A review reply is a draft. Approving it does not post it to the review site.

Tokens stay in the vault and are not shown again.
