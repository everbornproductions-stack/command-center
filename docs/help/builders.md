# Builders

Builders are in Create. A builder shows up only when it fits what you are doing.

Describe what you need. Preview which AIs would see your words. Then Build, Test, and Publish. Nothing is sent, posted, or charged until you click Publish.

HighLevel builders show up when HighLevel is connected, or when you mention HighLevel. They cover a lead form, a text or email you approve, workflow tags, a daily pipeline summary, and page copy. A text or email asks you to confirm permission, STOP, quiet hours, A2P 10DLC, and CAN-SPAM. Credit repair copy adds a CROA note and does not promise a result.

Accounting builders show up when QuickBooks or Xero is connected, or when a receipt or an invoice comes up. Meldmind only organizes them. For a tax question it says: Ask your accountant. It does not give tax advice. Sending to QuickBooks or Xero waits for your approval and does not pay the bill. Invoice Ninja stays off unless you turn it on. It is a separate service.

A website chat, a booking page, meeting notes, a contract, or a review reply shows up from that topic. A contract uses your own DocuSeal template. DocuSeal is a separate service. This is not legal advice. A review reply is a draft. Approving it does not post it to the review site.

Tokens stay in the vault and are not shown again.

## Web and marketing

Open **Create**, then **Web and marketing**. The page starts on **Suggested for you**. A builder shows up there when you talk about a website, a post, a reel, or an ad, when a project is a site, or when Netlify, Vercel, or a social account is connected. **Show all** lists the rest.

Each builder uses the same steps: **Describe**, **Preview the AIs**, **Build**, **Test**, and **Publish**. One button does the next step. **More options** holds page lists, service addresses, and tokens. Nothing is sent until you click **Publish**.

Site Builder and the Client Page Editor are Pro. Static export, Site Health Check, Ad Copy Studio, and SEO Fixer are Free. The Social Calendar and Email Sequences are Pro. Reel Maker is Extreme. Remotion stays off unless you turn it on, and it needs a paid license.

Site Health Check and SEO Fixer can show **Fix it**. That changes the page on this computer. Publishing to Netlify or Vercel, Postiz, listmonk, Mautic, or HighLevel waits for the click. With no token, Meldmind records a dry run and sends nothing. Tokens stay in the vault and are not shown again.

Email sequences always include your business name, a postal address, and an unsubscribe line. For a credit repair or insurance ad, check the boxes. Meldmind does not give legal, credit, or insurance advice.

## Build it all for me

Turn on **Build it all for me** to run every step in one go: describe, preview, build, test, and publish when it only saves on this computer. Meldmind answers the questions from your description. When a question has nothing in the description, it picks a plain default and lists it under **Assumed**, so you can change it later.

It still stops for three things, the same rules as Get it done: spending money, deleting something for good, and posting or sending in public. A second switch, **Also allow posting**, lets public posting through. It is off unless you turn it on. Boxes that only you can tick, such as a compliance check, still wait for you.

You see each step as it runs, and **Stop** ends the run before the next step. The switch is off at first. Meldmind remembers your choice for next time.

## Connect HighLevel in one press

On the Builders page, Connect HighLevel opens the HighLevel sign-in in your browser. If sign-in is not set up on this computer yet, the paste-a-token form opens instead.

Builder cards say "Needs Pro. You are on Free." before you fill anything in. When a builder writes posts, emails or ads, the same words check that texts get runs at Build and Test, so a promise or a left-over [NAME] shows up before Publish.
