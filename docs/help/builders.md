# Builders

Open **Create**, then **Builders**. The page starts on **Suggested for you**. A builder shows up there when you talk about a website, a post, a reel, or an ad, when a project is a site, or when Netlify, Vercel, or a social account is connected. **Show all** lists the rest.

Each builder uses the same steps: **Describe**, **Preview the AIs**, **Build**, **Test**, and **Publish**. One button does the next step. **More options** holds page lists, service addresses, and tokens. Nothing is sent until you click **Publish**.

Site Builder and the Client Page Editor are Pro. Static export, Site Health Check, Ad Copy Studio, and SEO Fixer are Free. The Social Calendar and Email Sequences are Pro. Reel Maker is Extreme. Remotion stays off unless you turn it on, and it needs a paid license.

Site Health Check and SEO Fixer can show **Fix it**. That changes the page on this computer. Publishing to Netlify or Vercel, Postiz, listmonk, Mautic, or HighLevel waits for the click. With no token, Meldmind records a dry run and sends nothing. Tokens stay in the vault and are not shown again.

Email sequences always include your business name, a postal address, and an unsubscribe line. For a credit repair or insurance ad, check the boxes. Meldmind does not give legal, credit, or insurance advice.
