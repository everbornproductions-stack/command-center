# Teams, People, and hosted runs

Teams is where you invite people and share the projects you choose. You pick a role for each person. The invite is a link you send yourself. The Teams page asks for a Studio license before you create a workspace. Pro is $18.99 a month. Extreme is $29.99 a month. Studio is the license the Teams page checks today.

People is a chat with your team. It shows up in the Help list only when that page is in your app.

Extreme hosted runs keep a schedule or a mission going in the cloud while this PC is off. That page shows up in the list only when it is in your app. Extreme is $29.99 a month. A hosted run still waits for your OK before it sends or spends anything. You choose the run. It does not start on its own.
