# Start a marketing campaign

Open **Start a project** on the Command home screen, or from the Settings menu. Pick **Marketing campaign** and click **Start**. The same steps run in Simple and in Expert. Simple keeps the route in plain words. Expert shows which AI was chosen and lets you edit the plan before it runs.

1. Describe the idea. A sentence or two is enough. Click **Continue**.
2. Answer the questions. Required: **What are you offering?** and **Who is it for?** Optional: **Where should it show up?** and **The one result you want**. Optional questions can stay blank. Click **Continue**.
3. Click **Ask the council for a plan**. The council builds the offer, the audience, a channel plan, and drafts you review before anything goes out. Nothing runs until you click **Build it**.
4. Read the plan. In Expert you can edit a step title or its instructions. Click **Build it**.
5. Each step goes to the best AI for that work. Progress shows on the live view and the flow map. **Open live view** is on the page.
6. On **Review**, read the result. Say what should change and click **Revise**, or click **Export**, or click **Finish**.

Export downloads a Markdown file on this computer. Nothing is posted or emailed. Check every claim, and replace anything marked [ASK], before you use a draft. Drafts stay here. Nothing is emailed or posted until you do that yourself.

To see a labeled sample first, click **Watch an example** on the Marketing campaign card. That sample is Northline Outdoor. See Watch example projects. To walk the rest of the console again, see Replay the tour.
