# What can Meldmind do

Meldmind is one window for the AIs you already pay for. You say what you want. It can ask one AI, a council, or a mission. You watch the work and you see the cost.

You can hand a job to another AI, such as a chat you already have open or Grok Bot. That AI can tell Meldmind when it is finished. You get a notice, and the summary shows in the job. If it cannot call back, use Paste result. Nothing from that paste is saved until you click Save.

A report back reaches only the person who handed off the job. Someone else's account cannot open it. The hub holds it only until your app picks it up, or for 7 days, and then deletes it.
