# What's new after an update

After each update, Meldmind shows a short What's new note. It lists what changed in plain words. The note shows once for that version.

Got it closes the note. Take the tour starts the short tour. You can also open this guide from Help any time.

Check for updates is on the Help page and in Settings. Nothing downloads until you click. Your settings, keys, and projects stay in place across an update.

Take the tour again any time from Help. Each step has one idea and a Try it button. You can skip the tour.
