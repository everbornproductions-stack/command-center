# Guided builders

A guided builder shows up for the job you pick and walks you through it one step at a time. You answer a few plain questions. Meldmind drafts the piece. You can ask for changes before anything is finished.

These are the builders this guide covers:

- A website
- Marketing and reels
- HighLevel
- Accounting
- Booking
- Meeting notes
- Contracts
- A chat assistant

Start a project is the page for a website, a marketing campaign, and reels when those choices are listed there. The same page can also offer a music video, an app, or a business plan. Other builders appear in the app when their own page is added. The list on Help only shows the ones that are actually here, and Open jumps to that page.

Opening a builder is free. Running the full job as a mission is part of Pro at $18.99 a month. Nothing is posted or sent until you click.

## Build it all for me

Turn on **Build it all for me** to run every step in one go: the idea, the questions, the plan, the build, and the test. Meldmind answers the questions from your description. When a question has nothing in the description, it picks a plain default and lists it under **Assumed**, so you can change it later.

It still stops for three things, the same rules as Get it done: spending money, deleting something for good, and posting or sending in public. A second switch, **Also allow posting**, lets public posting through. It is off unless you turn it on. Boxes that only you can tick, such as a compliance check, still wait for you.

You see each step as it runs, and **Stop** ends the run before the next step. The switch is off at first. Meldmind remembers your choice for next time.
