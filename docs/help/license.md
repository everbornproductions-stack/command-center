# License redeem

Settings has License and cloud. Paste a license key and click Activate, or paste a promo code and click Redeem. On this computer, Redeem can ask for your email. On the hosted app, the code redeems for the account you are signed in with.

A key works on 2 computers, a PC and a laptop. Settings, Devices, lists those computers. Remove one there to free a spot. Removing asks you to confirm first. If the key is already on 2 computers, activating another one says: This key is already on 2 computers. Remove one in Settings > Devices. Each person has their own key. An Extreme team seat is its own key, with its own 2 computers. A promo or tester code can be locked to one email. That code redeems only for that email.

The word license means the same thing on receipts and in some emails (some places spell it licence). The screen in the app says License.

Free includes 3 projects and 5 councils a month, 1 connected service, and 1 email account per provider. Pro includes every connected service and up to 8 email accounts per provider. Activating or redeeming a paid license raises those limits. Re-check asks the license server again. Meldmind does not charge a card from this screen.

Phone pairing and full history search are Pro. The router stays free. Shared learning and the scores dashboard are Pro.

A 14-day Pro trial can be started in the app. The trial is not started for you, and nothing is charged. When the trial ends you are back on Free.

Extreme joins Pro. Pro is $18.99 a month or $189 a year. Extreme is $29.99 a month or $299 a year. Lifetime for the first 100 founders is $349, then $499, and it closes after 500. Lifetime is Pro for life. Yearly prices changed from the earlier unpublished $159 figure.

Extreme adds early access, Max quality, and five team seats. Five team seats means 5 people total, counting the owner. That is the owner plus 4 invited. Early access means new features reach Extreme first, behind a flag. Max quality uses the best model each connected AI offers, with more reasoning and a longer context, inside the budget cap. Pro, Extreme, Developer, and lifetime pay links are published. A click opens a secure Stripe checkout with your sign-in email filled in. After the first 100, the $499 button stays on the page.

To unlock after you pay, sign in with Google or Microsoft using the same email you paid with. Meldmind finds your plan by that email, so there is no key to type. Press I paid, check now if it has not unlocked yet. The license key and promo box still work.

Before 40 founders have bought, the lifetime line says "Limited to the first 100 founders". After that it shows the spots left.
