# Licence redeem

Settings has Licence and cloud. Paste a licence key and click Activate, or paste a promo code and click Redeem. On this computer, Redeem can ask for your email. On the hosted app, the code redeems for the account you are signed in with.

The word license means the same thing on receipts and in some emails. The screen in the app says Licence.

Free includes 3 projects and 5 councils a month, 1 connected service, and 1 email account per provider. Pro includes every connected service and up to 8 email accounts per provider. Activating or redeeming a paid licence raises those limits. Re-check asks the licence server again. Meldmind does not charge a card from this screen.

Phone pairing and full history search are Pro. The router stays free. Shared learning and the scores dashboard are Pro.

A 14-day Pro trial can be started in the app. The trial is not started for you, and nothing is charged. When the trial ends you are back on Free.

Extreme joins Pro. Pro is $18.99 a month or $189 a year. Extreme is $29.99 a month or $299 a year. The lifetime founder deal stays $299 for the first 100, then $499, and it closes after 500. Lifetime is Pro for life. Yearly prices changed from the earlier unpublished $159 figure. New checkouts are not published, so nothing is charged from the app.

If you already pay $15.99 a month, that price stays. The old Square plan is not edited.

Before 40 founders have bought, the lifetime line says "Limited to the first 100 founders". After that it shows the spots left.
