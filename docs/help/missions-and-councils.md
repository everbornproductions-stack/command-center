# Missions and councils

A council asks several AIs the same question. They answer, review each other, and a chair writes the result. Free includes 5 councils a month. Pro is $18.99 a month and includes unlimited councils.

A council needs AIs from at least two companies. One key from one company is one AI, with no peer review. Two companies give you a council, and so does one OpenRouter key: Meldmind seats Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok through OpenRouter. See Council for the details.

A mission is for a bigger job. A planner splits the goal, specialists work in parallel, a different AI checks each piece, and an assembler compiles the result. Missions are part of Pro.

Open the chat box and type what you want. In Expert you can pick Council or Mission before you run it. In Simple, Meldmind picks one AI for you and says which one answered. When a council is possible, the answer offers Ask a council. Nothing starts until you click.
