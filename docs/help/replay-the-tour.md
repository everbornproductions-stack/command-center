# Take the tour

The tour is a short walk through what Meldmind can do. Each step has one idea and a Try it button. A step is hidden when that page is not in your app, so the tour never points at something missing. You can skip the tour any time.

The first time, a note says New here? Take a short tour. Click Start to begin, or Not now to leave it. If you skip it, a note says: Tour skipped. Take the tour from Help.

To replay the tour later:

1. Open Help and click Take the tour.
2. Or open the Help menu and click Take the tour.
3. After an update, the What's new note also has Take the tour. Got it closes the note without starting the tour.
5. Open What's new from the Settings page, or from Help, to read those notes again. That does not restart the tour.

Try it opens the page for that step. Next moves on. Skip tour closes it. Your chats, projects, and keys stay put. Nothing is sent or spent from the tour itself.

Start a project is the walk for one kind of work, such as a website or reels. Take the tour when you want the short overview of what Meldmind can do.
