# What can Meldmind do?

Help opens with a searchable list called What can Meldmind do? Type a word such as council, remix, phone, or team. Each row tells you the idea in one sentence.

Every row is marked Free, Pro, or Extreme. Pro is $18.99 a month. Extreme is $29.99 a month. Free is $0. These are the plan prices. Adding a licence happens on the Subscriptions page, and this list does not charge you. Open jumps to that page. Guide opens the article for that area.

A row is hidden when its page is not in your app. The list never points at a missing feature. Pages that are still being added, including some builders and Remix, show up in the list after they are in the app.

Take the tour again for a short walk through the same list. Each step has one idea and a Try it button. You can skip the tour, and you can replay it from Help. A tour button never sends, posts, or buys anything.
