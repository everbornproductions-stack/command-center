# Start a website

Open **Start a project** on the Command home screen, or from the Work menu. Pick **Website** and click **Start**. The same steps run in Simple and in Expert. Simple keeps the route in plain words. Expert shows which AI was chosen and lets you edit the plan before it runs.

1. Describe the idea. A sentence or two is enough. Click **Continue**.
2. Answer the questions. Required: **Who is the site for?** and **What should a visitor do?** Optional: **Which pages do you need?** and **Facts you can stand behind**. Optional questions can stay blank. Empty facts stay empty. Click **Continue**.
3. Click **Ask the council for a plan**. The council builds a page list, copy, and a launch checklist. Nothing runs until you click **Build it**.
4. Read the plan. In Expert you can edit a step title or its instructions. Click **Build it**.
5. Each step goes to the best AI for that work. Progress shows on the live view and the flow map. **Open live view** is on the page. API chats do not appear in claude.ai, grok.com, or ChatGPT history. Watch the work here.
6. On **Review**, read the result. Say what should change and click **Revise**, or click **Export**, or click **Finish**.

Export downloads a Markdown file on this computer. Nothing is posted or emailed. Replace anything marked [ASK] before you use the copy. Nothing is published from here. Open Live to see which step went to which AI.

To see a labeled sample first, click **Watch an example** on the Website card. That sample is Harbor and Rye. See Watch example projects. To walk the rest of the console again, see Take the tour.
