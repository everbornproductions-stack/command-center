# FAQ

## Where do I start?

Open the chat box and say what you want. In Simple, Meldmind picks one AI and says which one answered. A council needs two AI companies, or one OpenRouter key. Help is on the bar in Simple and in the Settings menu in Expert.

## Which AIs show under the chat?

The chips under the chat box list only AIs you have connected. Demo stays there only until a real AI is connected.

## Ask Meldmind says it is only searching. Why?

Ask Meldmind answers from these help articles using a connected AI (a saved key that was not rejected). With no connected AI, or if that AI cannot be reached, you get search only. It does not invent steps.

## The other AI finished. Where is the summary?

You get a notice, and the summary shows in the job. If that AI cannot call back, use Paste result. A line that starts with DONE: is enough. It is saved when you click Save.

## The installer says Windows protected your PC.

That is SmartScreen for an unsigned installer. Choose More info, then Run anyway. An antivirus warning for the same file is the same situation.
