# Start here: ask your first question

Type what you want in the box on Chat. Tap Send. Meldmind picks an AI and the answer shows under the box.

You can tap an example under the box. It only fills the words. It does not send until you tap Send.

Tap Read aloud on an answer to hear it.

Help is in the top bar. Type everyday words, such as how do I ask a question. Open an article and it shows at the top. Back to all help returns to the list.

An AI key is like a password for an AI account. Paste it in Settings when you are ready. Until then, Try it now gives a practice answer from a pretend AI. Nothing was spent.

Make text bigger in Settings, under Text and reading. Pick Large or Extra large.

Asking inside Meldmind does not cost extra money. You pay the AI company only after you add your own key. A practice answer costs nothing.
