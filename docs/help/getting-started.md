# Getting started

A new install starts in Simple. The first time you open Meldmind it asks: Keep it simple or show me everything?

Simple is one chat box. You say what you want, and the council picks the AI. Expert is the full console. The Simple / Expert switch sits in the top bar on every screen. One click changes it. Your chats, projects, and keys stay put.

## First open on a Mac

macOS Gatekeeper may block the first open because this build is not signed yet. If it says the app cannot be opened, use right-click > Open, then choose Open again. After that, a double-click opens it.

Settings, keys, and projects stay in ~/Library/Application Support/Everborn Command. Replacing the app in Applications keeps that folder.

## Top bar on a laptop

The top bar is one row of dropdown menus. A wider window splits them into more menus, and a narrower window merges them back. The row never wraps onto a second line. Resizing the window or moving it to another monitor updates the row right away. A 1024, 1366, 1920, or 2560 pixel wide window stays on that one row.

Start a project is in the Work menu, with Projects. The Settings menu keeps Settings, setup, the tour, and Check for updates.

Nova, Atlas and Sage are the demo AIs. They hide once a real AI key or a real AI is connected, and they come back only when every real AI is removed. Real jobs are not sent to those demo AIs while a real AI is connected. You can turn demo mode back on in Settings if you want the pretend AIs in the lists again.

Help is a tab in Simple and in Expert. Search the guides, or click Ask Meldmind. Take the tour again and Check for updates are both on the Help page. This note shows once after an update. Take the tour again any time from Help or from Settings.

Until you add a key, Demo can answer in the chat and nothing is spent. Demo does not answer Ask Meldmind.

## Learning from each job

Meldmind learns from every job you run. Anonymous track records help it pick the best AI for everyone.

A new setup checks I am willing to share anonymous track records. You can change this any time in Settings. On Who's best, clear I am willing to share anonymous track records.

## What we share

We share only anonymous track records: the task type, which AI, and the score. We never share prompts, content, or keys.

If you clear the box, this note appears: Meldmind learns from the community. With this off, your jobs won't help make it smarter for everyone, but you'll still benefit from what others share.

## Priority

Quality, Balanced, Max savings, and Speed apply to Single, Council, and Mission. Quality favours the stronger model. Balanced splits the difference. Max savings picks the cheaper model. Speed picks the faster one.

## Free models on this PC

A connected free model on this PC (Ollama, LM Studio, Aider, OpenCode, or another local OpenAI-compatible endpoint) is tried first on Max savings. Freebuff is not used. The route preview can mention a batch price. A run you send now pays the live price.

## Community track records

Add community track records from people who opted in. Quality, Balanced, Max savings, and Speed all learn from them. Nothing is uploaded. This stays on this computer.

## You saved

The top bar shows You saved this month and all time. It is an estimate based on token counts and list prices.

## Custom providers

Add a custom provider and tick Good at for the task types it should take. Matching tasks go to it. Scores from your jobs make the pick sharper. Leave none ticked and Meldmind learns from the name.

## Appearance

Settings, Appearance, adds theme presets, gradients, built-in pictures, and a photo from a file on this computer, next to the accent and the background colour. A web address is not accepted. Built-in restores the original background. A dim, blurred layer keeps text readable.
