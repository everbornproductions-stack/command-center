# Files, voice, and your ideas

Upload a file, a screenshot, or a document with an idea. Voice turns speech into text you can edit before Send.

Attach a picture or a video and ask Meldmind to modify it. You see the plan, then click Run. Preview, download, or save the result.

You can edit, delete, and re-prioritize an idea, add a comment, and set a status: new, planned, building, or shipped.

Marching orders has the same Upload and Voice buttons. Nothing is sent until you click Send.

1. Open the idea box in Settings, on the What's new card, or on the connections page when nothing is connected.
2. Click Upload for an image, a video, a screenshot, a PDF, a text file, or a Word document. Up to 4 files. A picture can be 8 MB. A video can be 12 MB. A document stays at 4 MB. If it is too big, the page says "That picture is over 8 MB. Make it smaller first.", "That video is over 12 MB. Trim it shorter first.", or "That file is over 4 MB." Paste a screenshot into the box if that is easier. The file stays on this computer. The note that can be sent lists the file name only.
3. For a picture, choose Retouch, Variation, or Describe. For a video, choose Trim, Caption, or Describe. Those buttons only fill the box. Click Show the plan. Meldmind picks a connected AI that can do that job: an image editor, a model that can see, or ffmpeg on this computer for a trim. Click Run only when the plan looks right. A demo plan says "Demo, nothing spent." You can preview the result, download it, or save it in a project. Nothing is posted. A picture over 3.5 MB can stay with the idea, but the page says "That picture is over 3.5 MB, so it can stay with the idea but it is too big to retouch."
4. Click Voice to record. Click Stop when you are done. Whisper on this computer is used when that program is installed. Otherwise Meldmind uses an OpenAI, Groq, ElevenLabs, or Gemini key you already saved. The words land in the box. Change them if you want. Nothing is sent yet.
5. Read the exact note, then click Send.
6. Under Your ideas, change the words, the category, or the status. Higher and Lower re-prioritize the list. Add a comment. Delete removes the idea from this computer. Those changes are not sent again. A picture or video on an idea can use the same plan and Run.

On the chat box, and on Marching orders, Upload and Voice work the same way. A picture or video there uses the same plan and Run. Edit the words, then send the orders with the usual button.

Do not put a password, an SSN, or a key in the words or in a file.
