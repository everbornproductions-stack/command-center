# Files, voice, and your ideas

Upload a file, a screenshot, or a document with an idea. Voice turns speech into text you can edit before Send.

You can edit, delete, and re-prioritize an idea, add a comment, and set a status: new, planned, building, or shipped.

Marching orders has the same Upload and Voice buttons. Nothing is sent until you click Send.

1. Open the idea box in Settings, on the What's new card, or on the connections page when nothing is connected.
2. Click Upload for an image, a screenshot, a PDF, a text file, or a Word document. Up to 4 files, about 4 MB each. Paste a screenshot into the box if that is easier. The file stays on this computer. The note that can be sent lists the file name only.
3. Click Voice to record. Click Stop when you are done. Whisper on this computer is used when that program is installed. Otherwise Meldmind uses an OpenAI, Groq, ElevenLabs, or Gemini key you already saved. The words land in the box. Change them if you want. Nothing is sent yet.
4. Read the exact note, then click Send.
5. Under Your ideas, change the words, the category, or the status. Higher and Lower re-prioritize the list. Add a comment. Delete removes the idea from this computer. Those changes are not sent again.

On the chat box, and on Marching orders, Upload and Voice work the same way. Edit the words, then send the orders with the usual button.

Do not put a password, an SSN, or a key in the words or in a file.
