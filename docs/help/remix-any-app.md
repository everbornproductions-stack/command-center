# Remix any app or game

Remix is for when you have seen an app or a game and want your own version of the idea. You show what it looks like and how it plays. Meldmind rebuilds it from scratch with your name, your characters, and your look.

When the Remix page is in your app, you can start from a screenshot, a short recording, or a web address you describe. Game starters are on that page when you want a game. A test step and Publish show up when those pages are in your app. You publish only when you click.

Remix is a Pro feature at $18.99 a month. Extreme is $29.99 a month and has no monthly cap. The What can Meldmind do list on Help shows Remix only when that page is in your app. Open it from that list.
