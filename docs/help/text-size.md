# Make the text bigger

Open Settings, then Reading. Pick a text size, from default to extra large. Turn on high contrast if the colors are hard to see. The change shows at once and stays after you close Meldmind.

Under a finished answer, Read aloud speaks it. Click again to stop. In the Finish panel, Read aloud and Print also work. Print uses your text size and puts a title on top.
