# AI employees and Test with personas

## AI employees

An AI employee is a ready-made helper with a job script. Twenty are on your team from the start: Receptionist, Speed-to-lead texter, Reactivation texter, Sales prospector, Customer support agent, Executive assistant, Social media writer, Review responder, Bookkeeping helper, Marketing strategist, Ad manager, Short video scriptwriter, Creative director, Website planner, Coding partner, Tax prep organizer, SEO strategist, Email marketer, Data analyst, and Hiring helper.

Open Agents. Under Your AI employees, click Use on one, say what you need, and it does the work with your own AI keys.

Words in [brackets] in a script are blanks, such as [Business] or [City]. Tell the employee in your message, or click More, then Customize, to make your own copy and fill the blanks once.

## Add more employees

Click + Add more employees to browse the library: quotes, invoices, contracts explained in plain English, YouTube titles, meeting notes, code reviews, and more. Type a word to find one, then click Add. Take one off your team any time with More, then Take off team.

## Where employees show up

- Chat in Simple mode: as you type, Meldmind suggests the best helper for it. Click Use to have that employee answer.
- Missions in Expert mode: pick employees under Employees for this mission, or click a suggested one. Each step goes to the best fit.
- Projects: each project shows a suggested team. Untick anyone you don't need and click Save team. Missions for that project start with this team.

## Test with personas

Under an answer, a mission result, a draft, a post, a page, or a design idea, click Test with personas.

1. Who reads it: the smart pick chooses the right people for this kind of output. To pick them yourself, open Choose people myself.
2. What they think: each person reacts in their own words, gives a score out of 5, and suggests fixes.
3. Click Apply on a fix, or Apply all fixes. You get a revised version. Click Use this version to put it back, Copy to copy it, or Undo last change.

The people are the Meldmind persona panel, such as Dana the bakery owner, Marcus the insurance agent, and Dorothy, who is 74 and new to technology. Their reactions are simulated by an AI. Nothing is sent or posted. Each test uses your own AI keys, and the cost shows in Credits.
