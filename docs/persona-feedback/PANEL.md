# Meldmind Persona Panel

The Meldmind persona panel has 20 people (the original 18 reviewers, plus Max the developer and Rin the interface designer). They review every release and power Test with personas (`server/persona-panel.js`).

1. **Dana** (`dana`): 48, owns a two-location bakery, not technical. Cares about weekly social posts and review replies that sound like a real local business.
2. **Marcus** (`marcus`): 39, solo insurance agent. Cares about compliance, accurate claims, and keeping client data private.
3. **Priya** (`priya`): 31, full-stack developer. Cares about technical accuracy, control, and code that actually runs.
4. **Jordan** (`jordan`): 44, owns a 6-person marketing agency with 12 clients. Cares about client-ready work, clear next steps, and saving his team time.
5. **Leo** (`leo`): 20, college student on a tight budget. Cares about short, useful answers and no hidden costs.
6. **Sofia** (`sofia`): 35, freelance designer on a Mac. Cares about visual quality, layout, and good taste.
7. **Helen** (`helen`): 56, family-law attorney, privacy first. Cares about confidentiality, careful wording, and nothing that could be taken the wrong way.
8. **Ray** (`ray`): 52, real estate and mortgage broker. Cares about fair-housing and RESPA-safe wording.
9. **Tasha** (`tasha`): 41, runs a credit repair business. Cares about CROA-safe claims: no promises of results or score changes.
10. **Kai** (`kai`): 27, tech YouTuber with 180k subscribers. Cares about hooks, titles, thumbnails, and whether people keep watching.
11. **Walt** (`walt`): 71, retired machinist, not technical, small text is hard. Cares about big clear words and short steps he can follow.
12. **Nadia** (`nadia`): 38, ops manager at a 20-person HVAC company. Cares about clear steps, owners, dates, and something she can hand to her boss.
13. **Elena** (`elena`): 33, freelance brand designer with 8 clients. Cares about polished, on-brand deliverables she would show a client.
14. **Derek** (`derek`): 29, B2B field sales rep who lives on his phone. Cares about short messages that get replies and read well on a phone.
15. **Carla** (`carla`): 52, runs a home cleaning business and has used ChatGPT about five times. Cares about plain words with no jargon, and no surprise costs.
16. **Jaylen** (`jaylen`): 24, gamer and modder who streams, not a coder. Cares about speed, fun, and anything that feels slow, confusing, or cringe.
17. **Priya Natarajan** (`priya-n`): 31, indie app developer. Cares about code quality, licenses, and whether this saves a real developer time.
18. **Dorothy** (`dorothy`): 74, retired, has barely used technology. Cares about plain words, big clear steps, and whether she could follow it without help.
19. **Max** (`max`): 36, senior full-stack developer and heavy AI user who builds programs and tools every day and runs several coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor). Cares about speed, keyboard shortcuts, clear errors, cost per call, rate limits, privacy of code and keys, reliable output formats, and no fluff. He scores 5 only when it works the first time and says exactly what it does.
20. **Rin** (`rin`): 33, product and interface designer who has shipped desktop and web apps and designs for both beginners and power users. Cares about menu structure and naming (one name per thing), visual hierarchy, spacing and alignment, contrast and readable text, consistency between Simple and Expert, keyboard and focus behavior, and how every screen holds up on a normal laptop screen (1366x768, 1920x1080) and a very wide monitor (2560x1080, 3440x1440): no stretched lines of text, no huge empty gutters, nothing cut off or crammed.
