## Purpose

The parties may share information so they can discuss contributing to, building, or evaluating Meldmind. Neither party is promising to hire, to pay, to invest, or to ship anything by signing this agreement.

## Confidential information

"Confidential information" means non-public information the discloser shares, in writing or orally, that a reasonable person would understand is confidential. It includes source code, product plans, customer lists, pricing that is not public, security details, licence design, and keys or credentials.

The recipient must not ask for, and the discloser must not send, a customer API key, a service-role key, a password, or a payment credential. Those do not become permitted just because this agreement exists.

## What is not confidential

Information is not confidential if the recipient shows that:

- it was public through no fault of the recipient
- the recipient already had it without a duty to keep it secret
- a third party gave it to the recipient without a duty to keep it secret
- the recipient developed it independently without using the discloser's information

## Duties

The recipient will:

- use the information only for the purpose above
- protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information, and never less than reasonable care
- share it only with people who need it for that purpose and who are bound by a duty no weaker than this one
- not copy it except as needed for that purpose
- not reverse engineer the discloser's source code except to the extent a written licence already allows

The recipient may disclose information if a law or a court requires it, after giving the discloser notice when the law allows, and only as much as the order requires.

## No licence

Confidential information stays the discloser's property. This agreement does not assign intellectual property and does not grant a licence, except the limited right to use the information for the purpose above. A contribution to Meldmind needs the separate contributor assignment.

## Term

This agreement lasts [two] years from the effective date. Duties on information received during that term last another [three] years after the agreement ends. Trade secrets, where the law protects them as trade secrets, stay protected for as long as they remain trade secrets.

## Return

When the discloser asks, or when the agreement ends, the recipient will return or delete the information, except for copies a routine backup keeps until it ages out, and except for one copy a lawyer may keep to show what was agreed. Those leftover copies stay confidential.

## Term and remedy

A breach can cause harm that money does not fix. The discloser may ask a court to stop a breach, as well as any other remedy. The recipient's liability is not capped in this template. An attorney should set any cap before signature.
