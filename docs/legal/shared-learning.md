# Shared learning

Meldmind is an Everborn product. Learning and sharing work in three levels: (1) on-device learning is on by default and never leaves your PC; (2) anonymous quality signals (**I am willing to share anonymous track records**) are on by default with an up-front notice on the welcome screen and in Settings, except in consent-first regions (such as the EU and the UK, chosen from the OS locale and region) where they default off and ask, while existing installs see the notice once and keep their current choice unless they never made one; and (3) content sharing for our own-AI program (**Share real prompts and answers for our own-AI program**, source-tagged, closed-AI outputs excluded) stays explicit opt-in and is never pre-checked. You can change this any time in Settings.

Meldmind learns from every job you run. Anonymous track records help it pick the best AI for everyone.

## What we share

We share only anonymous track records: the task type, which AI, and the score (counts only: provider, task type, thumbs up/down or retry/accept, cost and latency bucket, app version, and a random install id). We never share prompts, content, or keys (no prompts, answers, file names, emails, keys, or identifying ids). We do not share your name, your account, or the text of a chat unless you explicitly opt in to Level 3 content sharing, which is never pre-checked.

While the box is clear, this note appears: Meldmind learns from the community. With this off, your jobs won't help make it smarter for everyone, but you'll still benefit from what others share.

You can change this any time in Settings. On **Who's best**, tick or clear **I am willing to share anonymous track records**.

**Show what would be shared** lists the anonymous totals on this computer. This version does not send them.

Your own router still learns on this computer when **Learn from every job** is on. That local record is separate. Shared learning is only the anonymous track records above.

Anonymous usage counts are a separate switch in Settings: **Share anonymous usage counts to help improve Meldmind**. It follows the same regional rule (on with the up-front notice, or off and ask in consent-first regions such as the EU and the UK). It sends a random install id, the version, the operating system, your plan, and feature counts. It does not send prompts, answers, chats, file names, emails, keys, or identifying ids.
