## Assignment

Contributor assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in the Work, including copyright, patent rights, trade secrets, and any other intellectual property in the Work. The assignment covers the Work created before or after the effective date that relates to Meldmind, the command-center repository, or any product or service of the Company that Contributor contributed to.

"Work" means code, text, designs, data, models, prompts, documentation, and other material Contributor creates, submits, or has submitted for the Company, including pull requests, issues, and files.

To the extent a law does not allow an assignment of a right, Contributor grants the Company an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, paid-up licence to use, copy, modify, distribute, and sublicense that right, and to transfer this licence.

## Moral rights

Where the law allows, Contributor waives moral rights in the Work. Where a waiver is not allowed, Contributor agrees not to enforce those rights against the Company or its customers.

## Open source and other people's work

Contributor will not add code to the Work under a licence that requires the Company to release Meldmind, or to license Meldmind, under that same licence (including GPL, AGPL, and other copyleft terms) unless the Company agrees in writing first.

Contributor will not submit material that Contributor does not have the right to assign. Third-party material stays under its own licence and must be named in the pull request.

## Promises

Contributor promises that:

- Contributor is allowed to sign this assignment and to assign the Work.
- The Work is Contributor's own, or Contributor has named every part that is not.
- The Work does not knowingly infringe someone else's intellectual property.
- Contributor has not assigned or licensed the Work to anyone else in a way that conflicts with this assignment.

## Further steps

Contributor will sign any further document the Company reasonably asks for to record this assignment, at the Company's expense. If Contributor does not, Contributor appoints the Company to sign as Contributor's attorney for that limited purpose, where the law allows.

## No employment by itself

This assignment does not by itself create employment, a partnership, or a promise of payment. Any payment is in a separate writing. The Company does not promise to accept or to ship the Work.
