# Meldmind 0.4.25: findings from the 0.4.24 persona panel and audit

Source: a persona panel and audit run on Oct 27, 2026 against the v0.4.24 source (tag v0.4.24, read-only copy) with the real app server and headless Chrome, plus the 0.4.24 release check on the installed app. Nothing here changes code; this is the to-do list for 0.4.25. Full evidence (screenshots, JSON) is on Chris's PC in `Desktop\Everborn\meldmind-panel-0424\out\meldmind\` and the report `meldmind-dogfood\PANEL-AUDIT-0424-REPORT.md`. Screenshots are not committed.

0.4.25 scope already planned elsewhere (docs/flow/PLAN.md): read-only "talk to Meldmind about itself" and the in-app AI worker pool. The items below are fixes to add next to those.

How each item was checked: "Verified" = seen in a real run of the app (UI text or measured number). "Code" = read in the source only. "Not verified" = could not be run (no real AI, no real tray, no real cold start).

## Fix first (ranked)

1. **Light theme: sidebar group headings are nearly unreadable (Verified).** Expert sidebar, light theme, 1366 wide: Create, Agents, Library, My day, People, Business, Settings, Help are 11 px `rgb(123,133,149)` on the glass panel, contrast 1.7 to 2.9 to 1 (needs 4.5). "Work" is 2.7. The dark theme passes (11.8 to 16.9). Fix: darken the heading colour in light, or give the headings a text shadow like the dark fix in 0.4.24.
2. **Simple screen helper text is too faint in dark (Verified).** Colour `rgb(91,102,122)` on `rgb(38,39,47)`: 2.57 to 1 for "A page for your business.", "A picture from your words.", "About $0.01 or less"; 2.9 to 3.4 to 1 for "Pick what to make", "2 Tell us about it", "3 Done", "Quick picks". Also "Expert" toggle 3.98, "4 ready" 4.28, and white on the blue Chat row and Next button 3.65 to 4.0. Hurts Walt and Dorothy most. Fix: lighten the muted colour to at least 4.5 to 1 and use a slightly darker blue under white text.
3. **"Couldn't check for updates" bar is hidden in the new look (Verified in the test app, Not verified in the installed app).** In the Mac look the bar's Download and Hide buttons sit under the window header (`.mf-head` takes the click), and the bar is not visible in the screenshot; in the classic look (?mac=0) it shows. Fix: render the bar inside the Mac window below the header, or turn it into the quiet toast that retest 12 asked for. Also find out why the check fails on every launch of the installed app (release note 4, still open).
4. **"Engine is not responding" shows too early on a cold start (Verified).** With the engine stopped the full-screen box appears after 15.5 s (`ENGINE_TIMEOUT_MS = 15000` in web/app.js; the desktop watchdog threshold is also 15 s). A cold start takes about 30 s with many missions, so every slow launch shows the scary box with "Try again / Keep waiting". Fix: for the first 60 to 90 s after launch show a calm "Meldmind is starting up" line instead, and raise the box to about 45 s. Also keep reducing the real warm-up time (what still blocks for 30 s after boot is Not verified).
5. **A project's own Simple/Expert choice beats the saved global one (Verified, by design but surprising).** After a restart with global mode Simple, project "Bakery posts" (own mode Expert) opened in Expert and "Review replies" in Simple. Pressing Simple only saves to the active project (web/app.js setUiMode writes `uiMode` to the global setting and to that one project). So Chris sees Expert in a project he did not touch. Fix: show a short note "This project is set to Expert" with a "Use Simple everywhere" button, or make the toggle also clear the other projects' override. Persistence itself works (Verified: uiStyle, uiMode, uiModeChosen and project mode all came back after a real server restart).
6. **Two copies starting at once can kill a copy that is only slow (Code).** `resolveLock` in electron/instance.cjs ends a lock holder after 3 failed health probes (about 12 s). During a 30 s cold start a second launch (double click) can end the first copy with `taskkill /F /T`. Also the kill goes to the pid in `instance.json` without checking that the pid is still a Meldmind process, and a stale record after a crash plus a recycled pid could end an unrelated program. Fix: only end the holder if it has been silent longer than the cold-start time and the pid's exe path is this install's Meldmind.exe. Not run on Windows (no second launch was tried).
7. **Voice wording still lists OpenAI first (Verified).** The Talk bar says "Add a key to talk (OpenAI, Groq, ElevenLabs or Gemini)" (web/features/voice-actions.js:420) while the server message says free Groq or Gemini keys. Make the bar say "Add a free Groq or Gemini key". Spoken replies (ElevenLabs then OpenAI text-to-speech) and Instant voice still use a saved paid key by default (known follow-up in LESSONS.md; Code).
8. **"Use the 3 steps" chip overlaps "Settings and more" in the sidebar (Verified, 1366 x 800, screenshot G3).** Text collides in the bottom-left corner of the Simple chat screen. Fix: give the chip its own row or hide it while the sidebar footer is shown.
9. **Answer title is cut at the first full stop (Verified in demo).** Task "Write a week of Instagram posts for my two-location bakery. Save them as posts.md." showed the heading "...Save them". Use the whole first sentence or the whole task up to 90 characters. The answer also does not say where posts.md went (demo mode only, save-and-run with a real AI is Not verified; its unit tests pass on Windows: hotfix-04231 6 of 6).
10. **Graphics offer bar (Verified in headless Chrome, Not verified on real hardware).** "Your graphics look a little slow for the new look. Use the classic look? Yes / No" appears on first run and pushes the chat down. Expected on software rendering. Check that it does not appear on normal GPUs, and keep it out of the way (one line, dismissible).

## Verified working in 0.4.24 (no change needed)
- Mac look is on by default in dark mode and `uiStyle=mac` is saved.
- Dark Expert sidebar text passes contrast (11.8 to 16.9 to 1).
- Settings, per-project mode and look survive a real engine restart.
- Voice is free by default: `/api/voice/speech-paid` returns `{"paidSpeech":false}`; the free-first tests pass (speech 2 of 2).
- Engine stability tests pass on Windows (engine-stability 11 of 11).

## Not verified
Installed-app cold start and tray quit, real AI answers and real save-and-run, real GPU behaviour, anything that needs a paid or signed-in service.

## Audit results (0.4.24 click-through)
Run: audit-tools `sweep.mjs` (8 combinations: Simple and Expert, dark and light, 1366 and 390 wide) and all 33 flows, on a scratch copy of v0.4.24, demo mode, nothing sent. Ranked, most serious first. The code audit was a read of the v0.4.24 changes (electron/instance.cjs, electron/main.cjs, server/speech.js, server/private-rules.js, web/app.js), not a full re-read of the app.

A1. **Medium (Verified): notice bars sit under the new window header.** The hit test (`layout-matrix-keys`, `sidebar-keys-0421`) shows the "Your graphics look a little slow... Yes / No" buttons covered by `.mf-head`, and the start-screen buttons "Start something new" and "Open your projects" covered by the title (`#mf-title`) in 6 of 8 size and theme combinations. Same cause as item 3 above (update bar). Fix once: put `#notices` inside the Mac window under the header.
A2. **Medium (Verified): fixed bottom bars cover controls.** On the Start screen "Start" and "Watch an example" are covered by the dock input (`#cc-dock-input`) or the voice bar (`#vt-bar`) in 4 to 6 of 8 combinations; Projects "Continue this project" and "Delete" by `#vt-bar`; Images "New image" and "Edit" by the Help button. Some may be reachable only by scrolling; that was not checked per control (Not verified). Fix: bottom padding equal to the bar height.
A3. **Medium (Verified): Send is not the top element at 1024 x 768** (`topIsButton:false`); it is at every wider size. Check that the dock or voice bar does not sit over it.
A4. **Low (Verified): network noise in demo mode.** 0 page errors in all 8 sweeps. The same 13 failed requests in every combination (400 on /api/libraries, /api/agents/prompts, /api/builders/connect, /api/everyday-work/*, /api/reports/send; 402 on /api/brain, /api/teamrecipes, /api/remix/media). Expected without keys or a license. Not verified whether a real user sees a toast.
A5. **Low (carried from 0.4.22 U10, still open): silent buttons.** About 70 "dead" candidates per sweep, mostly tabs, filters and Thumbs (false positives). Real ones: Mic without a key and Images/Media "Generate" with no reason shown. Add one line saying what is missing.
A6. **Low (Verified): the News rail panel measures wider than the window at 1366** (elements at x 1422 to 1740) but there is no sideways scroll. Check it is not reachable by Tab while closed (Not verified).
A7. **Low (Code): owner-specific text in product code.** `server/private-rules.js` hard-codes "Everborn Cloud" and `G:\My Drive` as patterns stripped from every user's plans. Move to an owner-only setting.
A8. **Low (Code): speech.** An all-silence recording ends with "could not reach a free service" (`ALL_BUSY`), which blames the service for a quiet recording.

The lock-takeover item (6 above) is the one code finding to fix before the rest of this audit section.
