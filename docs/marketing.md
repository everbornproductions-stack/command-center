# Meldmind can use the apps on your desktop, safely

Headline feature for 0.4.9. Meldmind is the console for the AIs you already pay for. It can also use the apps already installed on your PC, including apps that have no API.

## Key points

- Works with apps that have no API. MCP is tried first. A website can be driven in the system browser. A Windows app can be driven from its own window. A screenshot path stays off until you opt in.
- Approval for each app. An app stays off until you turn Allow on for that app. You can turn it off later in Settings.
- Confirm before send, buy, or delete. The first click prepares the step. The second click is the one that sends, buys, or deletes.
- Stop banner. While an app is in use, a banner says Meldmind is using that app, and Stop ends it.
- No passwords stored. Sign-in happens in the app or in the browser. Meldmind does not keep the password, and it does not put a key on a command line.
- Self-healing when apps change. Controls are found by role, name, and shortcut, not by a spot on the screen. If a control moves, Meldmind tries the other ways it knows, then a manual handoff. You are told in plain words.

## First app: Google Antigravity

Google Antigravity is the first desktop app. Install and connect runs Google's official installer, then opens sign-in. You sign in once. Check sends one short test task. Add to Antigravity connects Meldmind as a local MCP server after you confirm. If the command is missing, the manual handoff is still there.

## Who it is for

People who already have several AI apps, and who do not want another credit wallet or a tool that stores their passwords. The same safety rules cover every app: allow it, confirm the dangerous step, and stop it from the banner.

## What we do not say

No income claims. No "it posts for you." Nothing posts, sends, or spends without a click. Square and billing stay out of this story.
