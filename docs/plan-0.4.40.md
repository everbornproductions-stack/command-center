# Meldmind 0.4.40: persona ideas committed to this round

Source: past persona ideas sessions (Oct 21 and Oct 27, 2026; files `Meldmind-Persona-Ideas-2026-10-21.md` and `-10-27.md`, kept in Chris's persona-ideas folder, not in this repo). Every persona reaction in them is simulated. This round holds the last 12 of the 42 past ideas. Eleven were in the Oct 27 file's "Ideas by persona" table only and never reached the master feature list. See also `docs/plan-0.4.37.md`, `plan-0.4.38.md` and `plan-0.4.39.md`.

Chris's rule (Nov 1, 2026): persona ideas always go into the next build round, never parked as "Proposed". Status of every item below: **Next round (0.4.40)**.

Rules for every item: nothing is sent, posted, signed or bought without a click and a preview. A free path comes first. Anything that spends the person's own AI credit shows the price first. The user's original files are never changed. Client data follows CLAUDE.md (no SSNs, dates of birth, account numbers or passwords in files, git, Drive or Supabase).

How to read "Done when": each check is something a person can see on the screen, in plain words. Each item also needs a test that runs on Windows and Mac CI (real waits, helper processes one at a time).

## 1. "We're closed today" notice (effort S to M)
From: Oct 27 session. Who asked: Dana.
What: one tap posts a short notice (closed, sold out, opening late) to every connected place.
Done when:
- The person picks a reason and sees every post in one preview.
- "Post all" posts only to places the person ticked; nothing posts before that.
- A reminder "Undo when we reopen" is saved.

## 2. Put my design on a product (effort M)
From: Oct 27 session. Who asked: Sofia.
What: drop a real logo or label file and see it placed on a mug, bag, sign, shop window or phone screen.
Done when:
- The person's file is placed unchanged (not redrawn) on at least a mug, a bag, a sign and a phone screen.
- Each picture can be saved as a PNG.
- It is different from "See it where it will live", which shows the real size of a post.

## 3. Open house sign-in page (effort M)
From: Oct 27 session. Who asked: Ray.
What: a QR code for the door opens a sign-in page with a consent box; afterwards each visitor gets a draft thank-you text.
Done when:
- A QR code and a sign-in page are made, and the page has a consent box that must be ticked.
- After the open house, each visitor has a draft text that passed the fair-housing check.
- Nothing is sent until Ray presses Send on each, or Send all. Visitor details stay on this computer.

## 4. Monthly client progress letter (effort S to M)
From: Oct 27 session. Who asked: Tasha.
What: for each client, a plain letter of what was sent this month and what changed.
Done when:
- The person picks the month, checks one sample, and the rest are made.
- No letter promises an outcome; the credit-repair (CROA) wording check passes.
- The letters are saved as drafts and nothing is sent. No account numbers or credit report details appear in any letter.

## 5. Comment digest (effort S)
From: Oct 27 session. Who asked: Kai.
What: paste video comments and get the top questions, complaints and five next-video ideas.
Done when:
- One box takes pasted comments and gives one result card with top questions, complaints and five ideas.
- The card is saved to MindMedia.
- A pasted comment list too long for one run says so before it starts.

## 6. Sponsor media kit (effort S)

Done when:

## 12. Call Meldmind (effort M, needs Chris's yes on cost)
From: Oct 21 session. Who asked: Walt, Derek, Dorothy.
What: a phone number to call and talk to Meldmind out loud.
Done when:
- Before any build, Chris gets a one-page note with the phone service options and their monthly and per-minute costs, and says yes or no. No phone service is bought or set up before that yes.

- If no: this item is closed and the reason is written here. Either way it is not left as "Proposed".
